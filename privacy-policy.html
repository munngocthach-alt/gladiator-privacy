Privacy Policy

Gladiator: Blood & Sand (Android)
Developer: Thach Nguyen
Effective date: 1 October 2026

This policy explains what information the game Gladiator: Blood & Sand (“the game”) uses, why, and the choices you have. The game has no accounts and no sign-in, and the developer does not run any server that receives your data.

1. Information kept on your device

The game saves the following only on your phone, so that it can work and remember your choices. It is never sent to the developer.

  • Settings: volume and the position and size of the touch controls.
  • Progress: unlocked levels, achievements, best scores and the attacks you design.
  • Your answer to the personalised-ads question, and whether you have bought “Remove ads”.

You can delete all of it at any time by clearing the game's data in your phone's settings or by uninstalling the game.

2. Advertising

The game is free and shows ads: a banner in the main menu and full-screen ads between matches. Ads are provided through Unity LevelPlay (formerly ironSource) and Unity Ads, operated by Unity Technologies, which may work with other advertising partners. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, these services collect information from your device directly, such as:

  • your device's advertising ID;
  • your IP address and the approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information, such as the device model, operating system version, language and the app's name and version;
  • how you interact with ads, such as which ads were shown or tapped.

The developer does not receive or store this information. The way these companies use it is described in their own policies: Unity Privacy Policy (covering Unity Ads and Unity LevelPlay).

3. Your choices about ads

  • Personalised ads: the first time you open the game, you are asked whether ads may be personalised to your interests. If you choose “Don't allow”, you still see ads, but they are not personalised. You can change your answer at any time in Settings → Ad privacy.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads on most Android phones).
  • Remove ads: a one-time purchase in the game turns off all ads.

4. Purchases

The “Remove ads” purchase is handled entirely by Google Play. The developer never sees your payment details. The game only receives confirmation from Google Play that the purchase was made, so that it can turn ads off and restore the purchase if you reinstall the game with the same Google account. Google's handling of purchases is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Children

The game contains combat and blood and is not directed at children. It is not intended for children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country), and the developer does not knowingly collect information from children.

6. Sharing and selling

The developer does not sell, rent or share any personal information. Information is collected only by the advertising and payment services described above, under their own policies.

7. Security and retention

Information saved by the game stays on your device until you clear the game's data or uninstall it. Information collected by Unity and Google is kept and protected according to their policies.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the European Union and United Kingdom, or under California law), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information, and to object to or limit its use. Because the developer holds no personal information about you, requests about advertising data are best sent to Unity using the links above. You can also contact the developer, who will help you.

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new effective date.
