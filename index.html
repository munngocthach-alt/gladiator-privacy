Privacy Policy — Brick Breaker

Effective date: 7 September 2026 Last updated: 7 September 2026

1. Who we are

Brick Breaker ("the Game") is developed and published by Nguyễn Ngọc Thạch ("we", "us").

You can contact us about privacy at gamevui.thestone@gmail.com.

2. The short version

  • We do not run any servers, and we do not collect your personal data ourselves.
  • Your game progress never leaves your device.
  • The Game works fully offline. Every stage, reward and screen is reachable with no connection.
  • The only feature that uses the internet is rewarded advertising.
  • The advertising component starts when you open the Shop or finish a stage, and it contacts our advertising provider at that point — before you are asked about ads, and whether or not you ever choose to watch one.
  • What our advertising partner processes, and why, is listed in section 4.

3. Data we collect ourselves

None.

The Game has no account system, no login, no analytics and no backend. Your progress, currency, purchases and settings are stored in a file on your own device. We cannot see it, and it is not transmitted anywhere. Deleting the Game deletes that data.

4. Advertising

The Game offers rewarded video ads. Watching one is always your choice, and if you never do, you can still reach every stage, reward and progression path in the Game.

However, the advertising component itself starts earlier than the ad. It initialises when you open the Shop or complete a stage, so that an ad is ready if you ask for one. From that moment it may collect the data described below — before the consent question is shown, and even if you never watch an ad. We describe this plainly because it would be easy, and wrong, to imply that declining ads means nothing is collected.

Advertising is provided by Unity LevelPlay (Unity Technologies / ironSource). Once the advertising component has started, it may collect and process:

DataWhy
Advertising ID (Google Advertising ID) and your "limit ad tracking" settingServing ads, limiting how often you see the same ad, fraud prevention, measurement
IP addressDelivering the ad, deriving approximate location, fraud prevention
Approximate location (country level, derived from IP address)Ad selection and reporting
Device and network informationDeciding whether and how an ad can be delivered
Ad interactions (that an ad was shown, completed or dismissed)Rewarding you, analytics and reporting
Crash and diagnostic data from the advertising componentStability of the advertising component

This data is collected by the advertising SDK, not by us. We never receive it: we are told only whether an ad finished, so we know whether to grant your reward.

Our advertising provider states that all of the above is used for analytics, fraud prevention, security and compliance, and advertising or marketing; and that the advertising ID, approximate location and ad-interaction data are shared with Unity, its affiliates including ironSource, and advertising partners. Crash and diagnostic data is collected but NOT shared.We use no third-party ad network adapters. The Game ships with the core mediation SDK only, which limits how many parties are involved.

Unity's own policy governs this processing:

  • Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy — https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy

5. Your choices

Advertising consent. The first time you choose to watch an ad, the Game asks whether your activity may be used to show you more relevant ads.

  • You get the same reward either way. Declining never costs you anything in the Game.
  • If you decline, we signal your choice to the advertising SDK as a refusal of personalised advertising and as an opt-out of the sale or sharing of personal information.

Device controls. You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your device settings (Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads). Doing so limits ad personalisation.

Playing without ads. You can simply never watch an ad, and nothing in the Game requires one. This does not stop the advertising component from starting — see the note in section 4.

6. Children

The Game is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. We signal to our advertising partner that this app is not child-directed.

If you believe a child has provided personal data through the Game, contact us at gamevui.thestone@gmail.com.

7. Data retention and deletion

We hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to retain or delete.

Data processed by the advertising SDK is retained by Unity under its own policy. Unity states it retains data "for as long as is necessary or as permitted in light of the purpose(s)". Unity publishes specific retention periods for children's advertising data (app interaction data up to 12 months; transaction records up to 180 days); it does not publish a single general-audience figure.

How to request deletion. Email gamevui.thestone@gmail.com and we will act on your request: because we hold nothing ourselves, that means passing it to our advertising provider and confirming to you when we have done so. You may also contact Unity directly using the route in its policy, linked in section 4.

8. Security

Your local save file is stored in the Game's private application storage, which the operating system isolates from other apps. Network traffic from the advertising SDK is sent over HTTPS to Unity's advertising endpoints.

We do not encrypt the local save file, and we do not treat it as confidential — it contains only game progress, and the Game is designed on the basis that a player may edit their own device's files.

9. International transfers

The advertising SDK is operated by Unity, which processes data internationally. Transfers are governed by Unity's policy linked in section 4.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data, and to object to it being sold or shared.

Because we hold no personal data, such requests ultimately concern data held by Unity. You can send any such request to us at gamevui.thestone@gmail.com and we will pass it on and confirm back to you; you may also contact Unity directly using the route in its policy.

11. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the "Last updated" date above and, where the change is significant, tell you in the Game.
